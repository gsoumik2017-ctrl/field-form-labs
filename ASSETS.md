# Fieldform Labs assets

The deployable site includes these current, local assets:

- `hand-study.svg`: editable hero hand illustration, with topographic contours.
- `topographic-lines.svg`: editable contour pattern used across the page.
- `tactile-contact.webp`: flat technical tactile sensing illustration.
- `physical-learning.webp`: flat technical perception and learning illustration.
- `robot-manipulation.webp`: flat technical robotic arm illustration.
- `favicon.svg`: editable blue Fieldform Labs symbol.
- `social.svg` and `social.png`: source and rendered sharing graphic.
- `manrope.ttf`, `manrope-500.ttf`, `manrope-600.ttf`, `manrope-700.ttf`: self-hosted regular, medium, semibold, and bold Manrope fonts.
- `Manrope-OFL.txt`: the font's SIL Open Font License. Keep it with redistributed fonts.

The three WebP images are optimized raster illustrations, not editable vector drawings. The separate assets archive also contains `source-artwork/flat-illustration-sheet.png`, the original full-resolution generated master. Its upper left, upper right, and lower left quadrants correspond to the three illustrations; its lower right quadrant is an unused contour background.

The hardware board image has been removed. All hardware imagery is conceptual and should not be described as actual Fieldform Labs product photography.
